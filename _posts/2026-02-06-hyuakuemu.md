"I am the smart one" thinks the child. The reason? She was able to succesfully recite facts force-fed at home in front of her class earning the praise of the teacher and the benign envy of her peers. This validation feels novel and rewarding. So she returns home and repeats the process. The next time a question or a math problem will be asked in class, she will be the first to answer. In time the ritual the gets internaliszed and she becomes a straight A student in middle school. With the highest grades she enters one of her country's top universities. However, soon she realises that she is just a one good student in a pool of great students. 

100 metres begins with two junior school student, Togashi and Komiya. To Togashi our, running is something where he is second to none and often gets hailed as destined to become one of the greats by his peers and teachers. Although just a mere sport, winning the 100 meters dash is something that validates Togashi's special existence in this world. This is evident from the brief but insightful exposition we get that much of his self worth is tied to his gift; that he is fast. Komiya on the other hand is a loner, whose unkempt hair, taped-up shoes and reticent demeanour reveal the hardships of a difficult childhood. Running to him serves as a coping mechanism; as the world gets blurrier and his problems move just a little farther away. 

Intrigued to find Komiya continuously running and sympathising with him over their shared interest, Togashi decides to help him win his first race at the school's sports day. Through much hardwork, Komiya is able to win this race, earning his first true taste of success. One day, Komiya challenges Togashi to a race, where he is beaten and injures himself in a bid to push himself to his limits. This is the last time Togashi sees Komiya in his junior school years, as the Komiya's family moves away. 

Fast forward to his High school, Togashi seems to be going through a period of stagnation and seems to have given up running due to an "injury". After attracting the attention of the school track and field club and having to participate in interschool events to keep the club afloat.
