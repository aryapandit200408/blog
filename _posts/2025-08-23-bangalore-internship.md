I was always drawn to digital design—the elegance of logic circuits, the precision of timing diagrams, and the satisfaction of watching lines of HDL turn into functioning hardware. What started as curiosity in the classroom slowly became a serious passion. So when I was fortunate enough to be selected as an on-campus intern at Texas Instruments, it felt like a defining moment in my journey.

This wasn’t just any opportunity, it was my first corporate internship. Stepping into the professional world for the first time, and that too at a company that has shaped the semiconductor industry for decades, was both exciting and humbling. The internship was based in Bangalore—India’s Silicon Valley—where innovation feels like a part of the air you breathe. Surrounded by cutting-edge technology and brilliant minds, I knew this summer would be transformative.
