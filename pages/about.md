# About

I’m Arya Pandit an engineer by training, a wanderer by instinct, and a collector of stories wherever I go.

This blog, Fables and Footsteps, is my small archive of moments that feel worth remembering: misty roads in unfamiliar places, photographs taken when the light felt just right, thoughts sparked by a book, a film, or a piece of music that lingered longer than expected.

I study Electronics and Telecommunications Engineering, where most of my days are spent thinking about VLSI, signal processing, and the quiet logic of circuits. But outside the world of silicon and signals, I’m drawn to stories like the kind found in travel, in thoughtful cinema, in music that feels like a memory, and in books that quietly rearrange the way you see the world.

I grew up loving worlds like Middle-earth and Westeros, stories like The Midnight Library, and films that remind us how deeply human we all are, whether it’s A Silent Voice, The Shawshank Redemption, or the quiet optimism of The Intern. They shape how I think about journeys, both real and imagined.

This space exists somewhere between those worlds. It is part travel journal, part photo notebook, part reflection on the things that inspire curiosity.

I suppose I’m a jack of many trades, master of some, always learning, always wandering, always looking for the next story hidden in ordinary places.

If you enjoy slow journeys, thoughtful stories, and the beauty of small moments, you might feel at home here.
